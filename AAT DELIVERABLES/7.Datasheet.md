# Datasheet — Tissue Parent-Roll Imports into Peru, 2020–2025 (SUNAT / Veritrade), v1.0

## Motivation
- **Purpose:** Assembled for my doctoral thesis to measure sustainable import patterns of tissue parent rolls (*bobinas*) that Peruvian converters import and convert into toilet paper, towels and napkins. The source records were created by SUNAT for customs control, not for research, so they contain no environmental variables.
- **Created by / funded by:** Angela Milén Acosta Ticona (UNMSM), from SUNAT customs declarations accessed through Veritrade. Self-funded doctoral research.

## Composition
- **What instances represent:** one product line of an import customs declaration (DAM).
- **Number of instances:** **2,994** records, January 2020 – December 2025 (HS 4803.00.90: 2,844; 4818.90: 97; 4818.20: 53). 73 importers, 20 origin countries, 123.1 thousand tonnes, US$ 138.0 million FOB.
- **Fields used:** date, HS subheading, commercial description (free text, six fields), origin country, customs office, transport mode, inspection channel, net kg, FOB/CIF value (US$), importer and supplier (coded).
- **Labels:** none in the source. Proxy label `sust` built from description wording: 133 records (4.4%) positive — 123 recycled-fibre claims, 20 certification claims (FSC/PEFC/ISO 14001/Ecolabel).
- **Missing data:** supplier not reported in 48% of records; inspection channel missing in 18; grammage could not be parsed in 10%.
- **Sensitive information:** importer and supplier identities are commercially sensitive; they are replaced by codes (IMP_001, SUP_001…) before any output.

## Collection process
- **How collected:** customs agents file the declaration; SUNAT records it; I exported the lines from Veritrade for HS 4803.00.90, 4818.20 and 4818.90, January 2020 – December 2025.
- **Who wrote the descriptions:** importers and customs agents, in free Spanish/English text, so wording quality varies.
- **Consent / ethics:** administrative records; no human participants.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labeling
- **Raw extract:** 11,586 lines (MD5 f80dfc93bd7849ce7eb762317f154682), covering all products under the three subheadings.
- **Step 1 – manual pre-filter by the researcher:** 3,748 lines kept (MD5 9db059eda20d8ec3b574817c0a828145).
- **Step 2 – rule-based screening (`cleaning/limpieza_reglas.py`):** a record is kept as a parent roll if roll diameter ≥ 60 cm, roll width ≥ 80 cm, or the text states industrial use (jumbo, rollo madre, para conversión, fabricación, materia prima); finished consumer formats (sheets, packs, boxes, 200-m dispenser rolls) and unrelated products are removed. Every removed line is listed with its reason.
- **Step 3 – expert review:** ambiguous lines were decided by the researcher (domain expert) and recorded in `cleaning/decisiones_manuales.csv` (26 lines added as parent rolls).
- **Final file:** 2,994 records, MD5 **f2d7e5e54cc455749e50e57bb96dbfea**.
- **Anomalies:** Isolation Forest flags 60 records (e.g. a declared US$ 795/kg, almost certainly a typing error); they are kept and tested in sensitivity analyses, never deleted silently.

## Uses
- **Appropriate uses:** research on AGGREGATED import patterns; replication of the method in other countries.
- **Should NOT be used for:** identifying, ranking or sanctioning individual firms; customs risk targeting; sustainability certification claims; commercial intelligence on competitors.

## Distribution
- **How distributed:** raw and cleaned files are NOT redistributed (Veritrade terms; commercially sensitive). Code, cleaning rules, aggregated tables and the MD5 fingerprints are shared on GitHub.
