# Model Card — Sustainable-Import Proxy Classifier (v1.0, trained on real data)

## Model details
- **Name / version:** Sustainable-Import Proxy Classifier (XGBoost), v1.0. Replaces the prospective v0.1 card (written before any data were cleaned).
- **Owner / contact:** Angela Milén Acosta Ticona <angela.acosta@unmsm.edu.pe>, Doctoral Program in Deep Tech, AI & Emerging Technologies, UNMSM
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Architecture:** XGBoost gradient-boosted trees (300 trees, depth 3, learning rate 0.05, class weighting) on structured customs fields, explained with SHAP. Pipeline: Isolation Forest (data-quality flag) → K-Means (import profiles, RQ1) → XGBoost + SHAP (RQ2).
- **Thesis:** Sustainable Import Patterns of Tissue Parent Rolls in Peru (2020–2025): A Machine Learning Analysis of Customs Import Records. Positivist paradigm.
- **Code:** `Bobinas_Sustainability_Analysis.ipynb` (seed 42; two runs give identical outputs). Code licence: MIT. Data licence: restricted by Veritrade terms (raw data are not redistributed).

## Intended use
- **Primary intended use:** Research only: identify which structured customs variables are associated with a proxy-based sustainability label in parent-roll imports (RQ2, H2–H3).
- **Intended users:** The researcher, thesis committee and policy analysts reading AGGREGATED results (MINAM, SUNAT, MINCETUR, PRODUCE).
- **Out-of-scope / misuse:** NOT for accusing or sanctioning individual firms; NOT for customs risk selection; NOT a sustainability certification; NOT transferable to other products or countries without re-validation.

## Factors
- **Groups evaluated:** HS subheading (4803.00.90, 4818.20, 4818.90); product type (towel, toilet, napkin, other); supplier reported vs not reported; year.
- **Known blind spot:** Customs records have no environmental field; the label measures *declared* claims (recycled fibre, FSC/PEFC/ISO 14001/Ecolabel), not true sustainability.

## Metrics
- **Primary metric:** PR-AUC (average precision), because the positive class is rare (4.4%). Also ROC-AUC, balanced accuracy, recall, precision and F1. Accuracy is not used.
- **Threshold:** 0.5 on class-weighted probabilities (fixed before looking at the 2025 test year).
- **Variation:** 5-fold grouped cross-validation × 5 repeats (25 scores) on 2020–2024; corrected resampled t-test (Nadeau & Bengio, 2003).
- **Baselines:** majority class and logistic regression, same features, same splits.

## Evaluation data
- **Temporal hold-out:** all 2025 records (599 records, 25 positive). The model never sees 2025 during training.
- **Internal validation:** 2020–2024 with StratifiedGroupKFold **grouped by importer**, so an importer never appears in both training and validation folds. (The protocol planned grouping by supplier; supplier is missing in 48% of records, so importer is used. This deviation is documented.)
- **Preprocessing:** encoding, imputation and scaling are fitted inside a Pipeline on training folds only.

## Training data
- **Dataset:** 2,395 parent-roll import records, 2020–2024 (108 positive). See the Datasheet.
- **Label:** `sust` = 1 if the commercial description mentions recycled fibre (RECICLAD*, RECYCL*) or a certification (FSC, PEFC, ISO 14001, ECOLABEL).
- **Features used:** HS subheading, top-8 origin country, customs office, transport mode, inspection channel, product type, log net kg, log FOB/kg, grammage (GSM), supplier reported (yes/no), month.
- **Features EXCLUDED (leakage control):** sustainability and fibre words, the description text itself, importer and supplier identities.

## Quantitative analyses
**Overall results**

| Model | CV PR-AUC (mean ± sd, 2020–2024) | 2025 PR-AUC | 2025 ROC-AUC | 2025 balanced accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Majority class | 0.045 ± 0.005 | 0.042 | 0.500 | 0.500 |
| Logistic regression | 0.475 ± 0.252 | 0.386 | 0.725 | 0.727 |
| **XGBoost** | **0.517 ± 0.238** | **0.415** | **0.853** | 0.696 |

- XGBoost vs logistic regression: corrected resampled t-test t = 0.63, **p = 0.53 → no significant difference**. Both clearly beat the majority baseline (PR-AUC about 10 times the base rate).
- 2025 test at threshold 0.5: recall 0.44, precision 0.28, F1 0.34.

- **Most important predictors (mean |SHAP|):** grammage, FOB price per kg, net kg, origin Vietnam, origin China, origin Indonesia.
- **Robustness:** removing the 60 anomalies flagged by Isolation Forest does not change the conclusions (see notebook, Section 9).

## Ethical considerations
- **Risks:** a proxy label read as proof of greenwashing; disclosure of commercially sensitive firm data; automation bias in policy use.
- **Mitigations:** importer and supplier names replaced by codes in all outputs; only aggregated results are reported; results presented as associations, not verdicts; no human participants.

## Caveats and recommendations
- **Caveats:** the label has one-directional noise (certified paper without wording is labeled 0). Very few positives in 2025 (25), so subgroup estimates are unstable. The model performs poorly for unspecified products and records with a reported supplier.
- **Pending validation:** two-rater manual labelling of 200 records (Cohen's kappa) and supplier checks in the public FSC/PEFC registries. The card will be updated with the kappa value.
- **Recommendation:** given p = 0.53, the simpler logistic regression is preferred for interpretation; XGBoost + SHAP is kept as a robustness check.
